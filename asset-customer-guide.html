01 / Start with your needs

Know what matters before comparing options.

When you are choosing a service provider, it can be difficult to compare businesses if the information is unclear or presented in different ways. Start by describing what you need, what matters most and what you want to understand before making a decision.

Useful first question
“Can you explain what you recommend, why it may suit my situation and what other options I should consider?”

Before you contact a provider

  • Write down the problem or outcome you want help with.
  • Note any practical constraints, timing or access needs.
  • Decide what information would help you feel confident.
  • Prepare questions about scope, price and next steps.

Compare like with like

Check what each option includes, what is excluded, what assumptions are being made and whether any price is provisional. Ask for unfamiliar terms to be explained in everyday language.

02 / Questions worth asking

Get clear answers before you decide.

What exactly is included?

Ask what the service covers, what it does not cover and whether anything depends on further checks.

What experience or evidence can I verify?

Look for relevant qualifications, genuine examples, reviews or references. Confirm that any credentials apply to the work you need.

What will happen next?

Ask about the process, expected communication, timing and who will be your point of contact.

What is the full cost?

Check what is included in the price, what could change it and when payment is due.

What if I have a question afterwards?

Understand the follow-up arrangements, support boundaries and how to raise a concern.

03 / Your decision checklist

Keep a record of what matters to you.

  • I understand the service and the options available.
  • I know what is included, excluded and provisional.
  • I have checked relevant evidence and credentials.
  • I understand the likely process and next steps.
  • I know the cost and how follow-up works.

Notes and questions

Take your time. A good decision is one where you understand the options, feel able to ask questions and know what you are agreeing to.
